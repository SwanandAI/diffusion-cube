# 100 Pathways — Adoption Companion

## What this is

A Next.js web app, the conversational companion to **100pathways.com**. Signed-in users work through one of two roles — **Explorers** analyse their own AI adoption against a corpus of real deployment pathways; **Contributors** turn their own deployment write-up into a new corpus pathway and publish it. Everything either flow says is grounded in that corpus (partly committed into this repo, partly community-published in Supabase), tracked internally on a 4-dimension × 4-stage grid. A public, no-login pathway library and an admin console for role/content management round out the app.

This file stays deliberately short and does not re-derive product/technical detail that already lives, verified against actual code, in the generated wiki below — load only the specific page(s) relevant to what you're doing, rather than everything up front.
