This Privacy Notice ("Notice") is published by [EkStep Foundation](https://ekstep.org) ("EkStep", "we", "us", "our"), a not-for-profit company registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013. This Notice explains what Personal Data we collect when you access or use the AI Diffusion Cube ("the Cube"), why we collect it, who can see it, how long we keep it, and what your rights are.

This Notice forms part of our [Terms of Use](/terms) and should be read alongside them. Terms used in this Notice — such as "Account," "Contribution," "Pathway," and "Library" — have the meanings given to them in the Terms of Use, and are explained in context below so this Notice can also be read on its own.

## 1. What This Privacy Notice Covers

The Cube is a web application that uses AI to help organisations learn from each other's real experience of adopting AI. This Notice is specifically about your Personal Data — information like your name, email address, or anything else that identifies you — and sets out what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. It applies to anyone who accesses or uses the Cube, whether you're browsing without an Account, or you've registered an Account to work through your own AI adoption or to contribute your organisation's experience.

## 2. Who Can Use the Cube

You must be 18 years of age or older to create an Account. If you use the Cube on behalf of an organisation — including to contribute — you confirm you're authorised to act on that organisation's behalf. The Cube does not knowingly collect Personal Data of children, and is not directed at them.

## 3. What We Collect and Why

### 3.1 Creating an Account

| Information collected | Why we collect it | Who can see this |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | To identify you as the holder of your Account | Only you and EkStep, unless you register as a Contributor and consent to share it with other Users — see 3.2 below |
| **Email address** | To create your Account, authenticate your login, and contact you about your Account | Only you and EkStep, unless you register as a Contributor and consent to share it with other Users — see 3.2 below |
| **Organisation** | To identify the organisation you use the Cube on behalf of | Only you and EkStep, unless you register as a Contributor |
| **Password** | To authenticate your login. Stored securely and not accessible to EkStep in readable form | Only you |

### 3.2 Registering as a Contributor

If you'd like to share your organisation's experience of adopting AI for others to learn from, you can register as a Contributor as part of the same sign-up process.

| Information collected | Why we collect it | Who can see this |
|---|---|---|
| **Organisation website (optional)** | To give context about your organisation on a published Pathway | Visible to other Users once published |
| **Organisation's role in the Pathway (e.g., Sponsoring Organisation, Program Owner)** | To help other Users understand your organisation's part in the adoption described | Visible to other Users once published |
| **Initial description of your Pathway** | To begin creating your Pathway | Only you and EkStep until published; visible to other Users once published |
| **Your declaration and agreement to our Terms of Use** | To record that you've confirmed your authority to contribute | Only EkStep |
| **Whether your name and email (from 3.1) are shown alongside a Pathway you've contributed to** | To apply your choice about being shown as a personal point of contact | Visible to other Users only if you consent to share it |

Your organisation's name is always shown once a Pathway is published — this isn't something you can turn off, since it's what makes a Pathway useful to others. Sharing your own name and email as a personal contact is always your choice.

### 3.3 Using the Cube's Chatbot (Explore, Analyse, and Contributing)

The Cube has a chatbot you can talk to — to browse and ask questions about Pathways already shared by others (Explore), to work through your own AI adoption (Analyse), or to build your Pathway by sharing documents and describing your experience (Contribute). All of this runs on Claude, an AI system provided by Anthropic.

Whatever you type, describe, or upload — questions, documents, anything else — is sent to Claude in full to generate a response or help build your Pathway. Make sure you have the right to share anything you submit, including if it happens to contain someone else's Personal Data.

| Information collected | Why we collect it | Who can see this |
|---|---|---|
| **Your conversation (Explore, without registering an Account)** | To generate a response to your questions within that session | Not saved — only you, during your session |
| **Your conversation, and any documents or material you upload (Analyse, after registering an Account)** | To generate a response for you, drawing on the Cube's Library | Only you and EkStep |
| **The Analysis document the Cube may generate** | To summarise your adoption, any gaps identified, and relevant Pathways for you to review | Only you and EkStep |
| **Documents, descriptions, or conversation you share to build your Pathway (Contribute, after registering an Account)** | To create or update your Pathway | Only you and EkStep until published; visible to other Users once published |

EkStep reviews a Pathway before publishing it to check it's structurally complete — this doesn't screen for Personal Data or verify accuracy, so make sure you're comfortable with what you've shared before it goes live.

We may also share anonymised or aggregated insights drawn from Analyse conversations with the relevant Contributor — for example, how many Users engaged with their Pathway, or what questions went unanswered — to help them improve it. This doesn't identify you.

### 3.4 Cookies

The Cube uses only a functional session cookie, to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising, tracking, or analytics cookies. The session cookie is deleted when you close your browser or log out, and does not track you across other websites.

## 5. How We Use Your Personal Data

**5.1 Account management** — to create, authenticate, and maintain your Account.

**5.2 Generating responses and creating Pathways** — Claude, the AI system described in Section 4, processes what you share in Analyse to generate a response for you, and processes what you share as a Contributor to create or update a Pathway.

**5.3 Publishing Pathways** — to review your Contributions and publish Pathways in the Library, and to attribute them to the Contributor organisation.

**5.4 Sharing insights with Contributors** — we may share anonymised or aggregated insights drawn from your conversations with the relevant Contributor — for example, how many Users engaged with their Pathway, or what questions went unanswered — to help them improve it. This does not identify you individually.

**5.5 Operating and securing the Cube** — to maintain, monitor, and improve the Cube's performance and security, and to detect and prevent misuse.

**5.6 Communication** — to contact you about your Account or the operation of the Cube.

**5.7 Legal compliance** — to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, or protect the rights, property, and safety of our Users, EkStep, or others.

We will not use your Personal Data for any purpose not described in this Notice without first informing you and, where required, seeking your consent. Your Personal Data will not be sold, rented, or used for advertising or marketing purposes.

## 6. How Long We Keep Your Personal Data

| Data category | Retention period | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Account information** | While your Account is active | To enable you to use the Cube |
| **Conversation history (for Users who register)** | Until you delete it, which you can do at any time | To let you return to earlier conversations |
| **Contributions and published Pathways** | Retained indefinitely, even after your Account is deleted | Published Pathways may already be relied on by other Users |
| **Declaration and consent records** | For as long as the related Account and Pathway remains published, and for a reasonable period after | Required for audit and compliance purposes |
| **System backups and security logs** | Up to one year | To support security monitoring, disaster recovery, and investigation of misuse |

Deleting your Account removes your account information and conversation history, but does not affect any Pathway already published from your Contribution.

## 7. How We Keep Your Personal Data Secure

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your Personal Data, including access controls, encryption, and logging of access to your data. No system is completely secure. In the event of a Personal Data breach likely to affect your rights, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

## 8. Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

**Right to access and correct your Personal Data** — you can view and update your Account information at any time.

**Right to erasure** — you can request deletion of your Account and its associated data. This does not extend to a Pathway already published from your Contribution.

**Right to withdraw consent** — you can withdraw your consent and delete your Account at any time. Withdrawal doesn't affect anything that happened before you withdrew.

**Right to grievance redressal** — if you have a concern about how your Personal Data has been handled, contact our Grievance Officer as described in Section 9.

**Right to nominate** — you can nominate another individual to exercise your data rights on your behalf in the event of your death or incapacity. Contact us through the grievance process in Section 9 to make a nomination.
