## 1. About the Cube

The AI Diffusion Cube ("the Cube") is a web application, available at [cube.100pathways.com](https://cube.100pathways.com), that uses AI to help organisations learn from each other's experience of adopting AI. It's part of [100 Pathways](https://100pathways.com), an initiative of EkStep Foundation, a not-for-profit company registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013. EkStep Foundation owns and operates the Cube.

The Cube holds a library of Pathways — structured, AI-extracted accounts of how organisations have adopted AI, including what they decided, what worked, and what didn't. Through a chat interface powered by Anthropic's Claude API, you can browse and ask questions about existing Pathways, ask questions and upload your own material for the Cube to work through with you, or contribute your own experience as a Pathway for others to learn from.

By accessing or using the Cube, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Use ("Terms") and our [Privacy Notice](/privacy). The Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, and protect your Personal Data and forms part of these Terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the Cube.

**3.4 Analyse.** Analyse lets you work through your own AI adoption — ask questions, describe your situation, or upload material, and the Cube will check it against the Library. **3.5 Contribute.** Contribute lets you share your own organisation's AI adoption as a Pathway, for others to learn from. ## 4. Disclosure of AI Use and Limitations

The Cube is powered by Anthropic's Claude. Anything you type, upload, or share — questions, descriptions, documents — is processed by Claude to generate a response or, if you're contributing, to extract and structure a Pathway. By uploading or sharing anything with the Cube, you confirm you're authorised to share it, including under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and rules framed thereunder.

Like all AI, the Cube can get things wrong. It may sound confident while being incorrect, incomplete, or based on a misreading of what you or another User shared. Don't treat anything the Cube tells you as the sole basis for a decision, especially one with real cost or risk. Verify anything important before acting on it.

If you're contributing, review the Pathway before it's published — the AI can occasionally miss or misstate details from what you submitted. Pathways aren't checked by EkStep for accuracy; that responsibility stays with the Contributor.
